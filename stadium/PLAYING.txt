SPEEDFEST STADIUM - how to play
===============================

1. Install TrackMania Nations Forever (free) from Steam or Ubisoft. The game races TMNF's own
   campaign tracks from your install; it never contains them. It finds TMNF in its usual folder
   (C:\Program Files (x86)\TmNationsForever or Steam's). Elsewhere: start the game with
   -- --tmnf="D:\Games\TmNationsForever", or set the TMNF_DIR environment variable.
2. Unzip this folder anywhere and run SpeedFestStadium.exe (Linux: SpeedFestStadium.x86_64).
   Windows may warn "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed: click
   "More info" -> "Run anyway".
3. PLAY races the campaign (your best times and ghosts are saved). ONLINE -> "Join the official
   server" races everyone else on 5-minute rounds of random campaign tracks; or type a server's
   address there.

Straight into a track: SpeedFestStadium.exe -- --race=B05

Keys: arrows / WASD or a gamepad to drive, Enter respawns at the last checkpoint, Backspace
restarts, Escape pauses, T chats online.

Options: graphics Low / High / Ultra (Ultra for strong graphics cards), HD textures, shadows,
sound, music and its volume, the ghost of your best run.

Music and sounds: open-licensed works by their authors; see CREDITS-music.md and
CREDITS-sounds.md. Fonts: Orbitron and Rajdhani (SIL Open Font License).
