# Third-party assets

Only **CC0** (public domain) assets are allowed, plus the OFL fonts. Every file that isn't generated
by our own scripts is listed here with its source and license. Generator scripts live in `tools/`.

| Asset | Used for | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orbitron (500/700/900) | UI headings and numbers | https://fontsource.org/fonts/orbitron | SIL OFL 1.1 (`game/assets/fonts/OFL-Orbitron.txt`) |
| Rajdhani (500/700) | UI labels | https://fontsource.org/fonts/rajdhani | SIL OFL 1.1 (`game/assets/fonts/OFL-Rajdhani.txt`) |

Generated by our own scripts (original, no third-party material): all sounds in
`game/audio/generated/` (tools/audio/*.py), including the dark-trap music (`tools/audio/gen_trap.py`).

## Textures (`game/assets/textures/`)

All PBR texture sets are **original and procedurally generated** by `tools/textures/gen_textures.py`
(numpy + Pillow, FFT-filtered noise and Worley cells, fixed seeds; no third-party images or
scans). License: same as the project (our own work, no attribution requirements). Rebuild with
`python3 tools/textures/gen_textures.py [asphalt concrete metal rock]` (~1.5 min), then
`tools/godot_import.sh`.

| Set | Maps (1024², seamless) | Tile | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| asphalt | albedo.jpg, normal.png, rough.jpg | 2 m | road deck (`road.gdshader`), city lots (`env_terrain` mode 0) |
| concrete | albedo.jpg, normal.png, rough.jpg | 4 m | decks, walls, pillars, footings, tunnels, sidewalks (`TrackBuilder.material`, triplanar) |
| metal | albedo.jpg, normal.png, rough.jpg | 2 m | barrier bodies, dark metal (albedo + detail), bright metal (normal + roughness only) |
| rock | albedo.jpg, normal.png, rough.jpg | 6 m | coast slopes and cliffs (`env_terrain` mode 2, triplanar), rock material |

Why generated and not ambientCG / Poly Haven (CC0): this environment's network policy blocks
ambientcg.com and polyhaven.com. CC0 sets from those sites may replace them later (list them here
with their URLs); keep the file names so the materials pick them up.

Import: lossy (WebP) with mipmaps (`compress/mode=1`, quality 0.85), so the web `.pck` grows by
only ~2.2 MB. Textures are skipped at quality 0 (`TrackBuilder.use_pbr()`).
