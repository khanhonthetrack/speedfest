# Tree textures: source and licence

From Poly Haven's "Jacaranda Tree" (polyhaven.com/a/jacaranda_tree), CC0 1.0 (public domain):
the leaves' colour with their alpha mask merged (`jacaranda_leaves.png`, 1024 px), their normal
map, the trunk's colour and normal map (512 px). Used by `env/tm_trees.gd` on leaf cards and bark.
