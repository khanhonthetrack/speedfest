# Third-party assets

Only **CC0** (public domain) assets are allowed, plus the OFL fonts. Every file that isn't generated
by our own scripts is listed here with its source and license. Generator scripts live in `tools/`.
(The Stadium remake also takes owner-chosen assets under other licenses, each listed in its own
`stadium/assets/*/CREDITS.md`; nothing that forbids redistribution goes in, since the repo is public.)

|---|---|---|---|

| Universal Animation Library [Standard] | humanoid animation (43 clips + a Mannequin), `stadium/assets/anim/ual/` | Quaternius, https://quaternius.com | CC0 1.0 (`stadium/assets/anim/ual/LICENSE-CC0.txt`) |

## Humanoid animation

Four packs arrived from the owner on 23 Oct 2026; **`docs/ANIMATIONS.md`** studies all four (every
clip, its length, its skeleton, its root motion, and what Godot 4.7.2 needs to import it) and
`stadium/assets/anim/CREDITS.md` lists their licenses. Only the CC0 one is in the repo. The three
that may be used in the game but not redistributed stay with the owner and stage back with
`tools/anim/stage_packs.sh`; their inventories are kept in `tools/anim/manifests/*.json`.

Generated by our own scripts (original, no third-party material): all sounds in
`game/audio/generated/` (tools/audio/*.py), including the dark-trap music (`tools/audio/gen_trap.py`).

## Textures (`game/assets/textures/`, `game/assets/hd/textures/`)

The PBR sets are **CC0 photo scans from ambientCG**, fetched and converted by
`tools/textures/fetch_cc0.py` (keeps the file names below; albedo scaled per channel and roughness
scaled to the means the materials were tuned for; asphalt and metal get a contrast / normal-strength
boost so the fine scans still read at driving distance). Rebuild with
`python3 tools/textures/fetch_cc0.py --hd` (~20 s, needs ambientcg.com), then `tools/godot_import.sh`.
The original generator `tools/textures/gen_textures.py` (our own procedural sets, no third-party
material) stays as the offline fallback and writes the same file names.

| Set | Source (CC0) | Tile | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| asphalt | Asphalt 031, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Asphalt031 | 2 m | road deck (`road.gdshader`), city lots (`env_terrain` mode 0) |
| concrete | Concrete 034, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034 | 4 m | decks, walls, pillars, footings, tunnels, sidewalks (`TrackBuilder.material`, triplanar) |
| metal | Metal 038, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal038 | 2 m | barrier bodies, dark metal (albedo + detail), bright metal (normal + roughness only) |
| rock | Rock 022, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rock022 | 6 m | coast slopes and cliffs (`env_terrain` mode 2, triplanar), rock material |

License: CC0 1.0 (public domain), https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/. No attribution required;
listed here anyway.

- `game/assets/textures/<set>/`: 1K (1024², seamless) `<set>_albedo.jpg`, `<set>_normal.png`
  (OpenGL convention, ambientCG NormalGL), `<set>_rough.jpg`. Shipped everywhere, including web.
  Import: lossy WebP with mipmaps (`compress/mode=1`, quality 0.85): the web `.pck` is ~8.4 MB.
- `game/assets/hd/textures/<set>/`: 2K versions of the same scans (normal map as `.jpg`, since the
  scans are JPEG anyway). Desktop only: the Web preset excludes `assets/hd/*`, and
  `TrackBuilder.pbr_texture()` prefers them on Forward+ at quality ≥ 2 (High/Ultra). Import: VRAM
  compressed, high quality, mipmaps; `project.godot` imports both S3TC/BPTC (PC) and ETC2/ASTC
  (ARM, Switch), so the committed `.import` files work on any editor machine.
- Textures are skipped at quality 0 (`TrackBuilder.use_pbr()`).

## Sky panoramas (`game/assets/sky/`, `game/assets/hd/sky/`)

**CC0 HDRIs from Poly Haven**, fetched and converted by `tools/textures/fetch_hdri.py` (session 7).
The script downloads each 2K Radiance HDR and scales it so the 99.7th luminance percentile sits just
below white. It writes sRGB 8-bit JPEG equirect panoramas: the sun disk is drawn by the sky shader,
so clipping the photo's sun costs nothing. It also generates `game/tracks/scenery/sky_panoramas.gd`
(the scale to undo, and where the sun or moon is). Rebuild with `python3 tools/textures/fetch_hdri.py`
(~10 s, needs polyhaven.com and ffmpeg), then `tools/godot_import.sh`.

| Theme | Source (CC0) | Authors | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| city | Kloppenheim 07 (Pure Sky), https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_07_puresky | Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest | Neon city: overcast night, graded violet, lit from below by the procedural city glow |
| arena | Kloppenheim 02 (Pure Sky), https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_02_puresky | Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest | Stadium: clear moonlit night (the moon sits behind the key light), sharp procedural stars on top |
| coast | Belfast Sunset (Pure Sky), https://polyhaven.com/a/belfast_sunset_puresky | Greg Zaal, Dimitrios Savva, Jarod Guest | Dusk coast: low sun under a cloud deck, aligned with the scene's sun; zenith graded deep violet |

License: CC0 1.0 (public domain), https://polyhaven.com/license. No attribution required; listed
anyway.

- `game/assets/sky/<theme>_sky.jpg`: 1024×512 (40–60 KB), every build including web.
  `game/assets/hd/sky/<theme>_sky.jpg`: 2048×1024 (140–180 KB), desktop only (Forward+, quality ≥ 2).
  Import: lossy WebP at quality 0.9 with no mipmaps (the shader samples LOD 0 so the equirect seam
  doesn't pick a blurry mip).
- `SceneryEnv._panorama()` blends the photo over the procedural sky (`env_sky.gdshader` `pano_*`
  uniforms: exposure relative to the photo's real brightness, tint, saturation, zenith grade, how
  much procedural glow/stars stay on top). Below the horizon the procedural ground stays, so the
  dark ground keeps the ambient light. Quality 0 keeps the procedural sky only.
