SPEEDFEST STADIUM - how to play
===============================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere and run SpeedFestStadium.exe (Linux: SpeedFestStadium.x86_64).
   Nothing else to install. Windows may warn "Windows protected your PC" because the game
   isn't signed: click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
2. SOLO -> CAMPAIGN races SpeedFest's own campaign: 65 tracks in five series (White, Green,
   Blue, Red, Black), each with author, gold, silver and bronze times. Your best times and the
   ghost of your best run are saved. SOLO -> REPLAYS plays back the runs you saved.
3. ONLINE: log in or register to climb the ladder and keep online records, or play as a guest.
   Pick a server from the list and JOIN (or SPECTATE), or type its address under JOIN BY
   ADDRESS. World and zone rankings are there too.
4. EDITORS -> TRACK EDITOR: build your own track from the Stadium's blocks and drive it to the
   finish to validate it. PROFILE sets your nickname (TrackMania's $-colour codes work), zone
   and car colour.

TrackMania tracks too: SOLO -> OPEN A TRACK FILE races any TrackMania Nations Forever track file
(.Challenge.Gbx), yours or downloaded.

Straight into a track: SpeedFestStadium.exe -- --race=A01

Keys: arrows / WASD or a gamepad to drive, Enter respawns at the last checkpoint, Backspace
restarts, Escape pauses. Online: T chats, V opens the votes (F1 yes, F2 no), F3 spectates.

Options: graphics Low / High / Ultra (Ultra for strong graphics cards), HD textures, shadows,
sound, music and its volume, the ghost of your best run.
