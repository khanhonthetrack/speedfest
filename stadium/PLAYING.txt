SPEEDFEST STADIUM - how to play
===============================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere and run SpeedFestStadium.exe (Linux: SpeedFestStadium.x86_64).
   Nothing else to install. Windows may warn "Windows protected your PC" because the game
   isn't signed: click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

NEW IN 0.9
- SKY: a seventh series of ten tracks raced 200-440 m above the city's streets, between
  skyscrapers. Ride walls and the faces of towers, drive up one side of a skyscraper and down
  the other, spiral round a tower, roll upside down, jump roof to roof, ski-jump off the tallest
  tower. Where the road glows violet it is mag-road: it holds the car like a magnet, on walls and
  upside down. Three tracks have two finishes: a safe way and a faster, riskier jump. Fall off and
  you respawn at the last checkpoint by yourself. Brake before a roof's edge: over it the road only
  holds you at about 130-170 km/h.
- The GARAGE: sixteen road cars of our own with five liveries each, and our Le Mans hypercar.
  Tuning: spoilers, rims, underglow and hoods, ten of each.
- New menus of our own look: tabs along the top (Home, Solo, Multiplayer, Garage, Community,
  Editor), your profile at the top right.

2. SOLO races SpeedFest's own campaign: 80 tracks in seven series (White, Green, Blue, Red,
   Black, City and Sky), each with author, gold, silver and bronze times. The City series races
   through a neon city of skyscrapers instead of the Stadium; three of its five tracks are at
   night: wet streets mirroring the neon, street lights, searchlights, an airship overhead. The
   Sky series is high above that city, eight of its ten tracks at night (graphics on Ultra for the
   reflections and the glow). Your best times and the ghost of your best run are saved.
   SOLO -> REPLAYS plays back the runs you saved. Before your first run on a track (or Escape ->
   SELECT OPPONENTS) pick the ghost you race against: a gold, silver or bronze medal run, or your
   personal best.
3. MULTIPLAYER: log in or register to climb the ladder and keep online records, or play as a
   guest. Pick a server from the list and JOIN (or SPECTATE), or type its address under JOIN BY
   ADDRESS. World and zone rankings are there too.
4. GARAGE: pick your car and its livery, then TUNING for its spoiler, rims, underglow and hood.
   EDITOR: build your own track from the Stadium's blocks and drive it to the finish to validate
   it. Your profile (top right) sets your nickname (TrackMania's $-colour codes work) and zone.
5. COMMUNITY: TrackMania players' cars (ManiaPark) and tracks (TrackMania Exchange), shown by
   their pictures. Download a car and pick "Drive this car" to race with it (online too), or
   download a track and Play it. TEXTURE MODS re-skins the Stadium with a mod made for
   TrackMania. What you download stays under My Cars and My Tracks.

TrackMania tracks too: SOLO -> BROWSE races any TrackMania Nations Forever track file
(.Challenge.Gbx), yours or downloaded.

Straight into a track: SpeedFestStadium.exe -- --race=G05

Keys: arrows / WASD or a gamepad to drive, Enter respawns at the last checkpoint, Backspace
restarts, Escape pauses, 1 / 2 / 3 change the camera (close behind, far behind, the cockpit).
Online: T chats, V opens the votes (F1 yes, F2 no), F3 spectates.

Options: graphics Low / High / Ultra (Ultra for strong graphics cards), HD textures, shadows,
sound and music and their volumes, the ghost of your best run. The bar along the top of the
screen (in a race: move the mouse to the top edge) has the volumes, the display settings and
the clock too.

Music and sounds: open-licensed works by their authors; see CREDITS-music.md and
CREDITS-sounds.md. The city's buildings and street lights: see CREDITS-city.md. Fonts: Montserrat,
Cabin, Orbitron, Rajdhani and DSEG (SIL Open Font License).
