# Story mode: "Rear View" (draft)

> Status: design draft, nothing built. The owner's idea (TRANSCRIPT, session 11), polished into ten
> chapters. Names, numbers and the build list are proposals until the owner signs them off.

## 1. The idea

A story-driven, choices-matter campaign in the style of *As Dusk Falls* and *Life is Strange*, played
as Trackmania time attack. Ten chapters, three maps each. The story runs **backwards** like *Memento*:
each chapter starts before the previous one. You open in a traffic jam with a crying woman and a black
car that isn't yours. You finish two years earlier, on the night you took it.

The question the whole game asks: **who is the man in the car?** A killer who collects dead men's
cars, a husband who keeps his family by keeping a secret, or a man who has just lost everything. Your
choices decide which.

Title idea: **Rear View**. You only ever see this story in the mirror.

## 2. How it plays

- **10 chapters x 3 maps = 30 maps.** Earn **gold** on a map to unlock the next. **Author** is
  optional and unlocks a memory fragment (see §6).
- **Choice maps** (usually the third map of a chapter): the road forks before the end and each branch
  has its own finish gate, with its own medal times. The finish of **the run you hold when you press
  CONTINUE** is your decision. You can retry and switch as often as you like until you leave the map,
  then it's locked.
- The routes feel different. The dark route is usually short and dangerous (no barriers, along a
  drop). The other route is longer and more technical. Each one needs real skill for gold.
- **Story while you drive:** on your first run of a map, dialogue appears as subtitles over the HUD,
  and phone messages come in between maps, as in NFSU2. Short scenes open and close each chapter.
- **The golden rule of the reverse structure:** *choices never change what you've already seen;
  they decide what it meant.* Scenes from later in time (played earlier) are shot so they stay true
  for every choice. The woman's face is never shown, the voices are text, and the details stay out of frame.
- **Reverse progression:** you start the game in the most powerful car (the X) and end it in your old
  Kaze RS. You lose cars as you go back in time.
- **The weather tells the time:** the present is rainy night in the city. The further back you go, the
  drier and warmer the light gets, ending on a sunset coast road.

## 3. Cast

| who | what |
|---|---|
| **Nico** | The main character. Mechanic, husband, father, ex-street racer. His workshop was going under two years ago. |
| **Clara** | His wife. Drives a Pixie S. |
| **Ella** | Their daughter, 7 tonight (5 in chapter 10). |
| **Lena** | A mechanic from the night scene. She built the X's engine for Saint, knew the car the moment she saw it, and keeps Nico's secret. The only person he can talk to about the nights. |
| **SHE** | The woman in the passenger seat in chapters 1-2. Her face is never shown until the finale. She is **Clara or Lena**, depending on your choices. |
| **Saint** | The final boss. Two years ago, the king of the coast roads and the owner of the X. |
| **The X** | Saint's one-of-a-kind black car. Nico drives it in chapters 1-8. It has an unrepaired dent in the passenger door. |
| **Four bosses** | Rivals who race Nico for pink slips: **Big Joe** (Goliath HD), **The Countess** (Veloce GT), **Fox** (Kitsune R4), **Hammer** (Brute V8). Win and their car goes into Nico's garage either way. Whether they walk away is your choice. |

## 4. The ten chapters (in play order)

Each chapter ends where the one before it began.

### Chapter 1: "Gridlock". Tonight, 23:40 (city, rain)
A jammed ring road. Nico at the wheel of the X, SHE in the passenger seat, rain on the glass. *"You
told me it was over."* She gets out and walks away between the stopped cars, crying. The camera
lingers on a dent in the passenger door. The jam clears.
- **1-1 Gridlock:** a floating road above the frozen traffic, with jumps over the jammed lanes.
- **1-2 Off-ramp:** down off the ring road into the city.
- **1-3 Three Ways (CHOICE):** three roads, three finish gates.
  - **GARAGE:** the lockup on the industrial estate, where he stores the X with a lot of other cars.
  - **HOME:** the suburb, his wife and daughter.
  - **PUB:** the old town, a beer and being sad.

  Only the arrival is shown, cut before it means anything. This is where Nico *wants* to go.

### Chapter 2: "Pink Slip: Hammer". Earlier tonight, 21:00 (stadium)
SHE is in the passenger seat at an underground meet. Hammer puts his Brute V8 on the line against the X.
- **2-1 The Meet**, **2-2 Qualifier**
- **2-3 Hammer (CHOICE):** **PUSH** (the inside line along the edge, door to door) or **CLEAN** (the long
  outside line). As he loses, Hammer shouts *"Ask him how Saint died!"* She heard. They drive off
  onto the ring road and into chapter 1.

### Chapter 3: "Seven Candles". One week earlier (city, dusk to night)
Ella's 7th birthday. Lena calls: Hammer's challenge must be answered tonight, or the whole scene will
say the X's driver is scared.
- **3-1 Errands**, **3-2 Across Town**
- **3-3 Seven Candles (CHOICE):** **HOME** (the party, Clara) or **THE MEET** (Lena, accept the challenge).

### Chapter 4: "Pink Slip: Fox". One month earlier (coast canyon: dirt and gravel)
Fox, the rally queen, on her own canyon stage. Lena spots for Nico over the radio.
- **4-1 Canyon Road**, **4-2 Fox's Stage** (her ghost shows the line)
- **4-3 Fox (CHOICE):** **PUSH** (the cliff-side line) or **CLEAN** (the long way round).

### Chapter 5: "The Papers". Three months earlier (city)
Clara finds the X's registration in the glovebox, with a name she half-remembers from the news.
- **5-1 School Run**, **5-2 Rush Hour**
- **5-3 The Papers (CHOICE):** **HOME** (face Clara; she gets the truth piece by piece) or
  **LENA'S** (run to the one person who already knows).

### Chapter 6: "Pink Slip: The Countess". Six months earlier (stadium, floodlights)
The Countess and her Veloce GT: the stadium's fastest. Top-speed maps, big jumps.
- **6-1 Floodlights**, **6-2 Her Track**
- **6-3 The Countess (CHOICE):** **PUSH** or **CLEAN**.

### Chapter 7: "Lena". Nine months earlier (city rooftops)
Nico meets Lena. She recognises the X before he's even parked: she built its engine. She doesn't ask
where Saint is. She already knows.
- **7-1 Rooftops**, **7-2 Her Workshop**
- **7-3 Lena (CHOICE):** **LENA'S PLACE** (stay) or **HOME**.

### Chapter 8: "Pink Slip: Big Joe". One year earlier (coast dunes: boosts and jumps)
The first challenger. The scene wants to know who's driving Saint's car. This is the first time Nico
chooses whether to do it again.
- **8-1 Dune Road**, **8-2 Big Joe's Jumps**
- **8-3 Big Joe (CHOICE):** **PUSH** or **CLEAN**.

### Chapter 9: "Headlines". Two years earlier, the morning after (coast to city, no choice)
The radio: *"A street racer was killed overnight on the coast road."* Nico drives the dented X off the
coast, past police lights on the beach below, and hides it in a rented lockup: the first car of
the collection. Then he takes his old Kaze RS home. Clara is making breakfast. Ella shows him a
drawing. He says nothing.
- **9-1 Headlines** (the X), **9-2 The Lockup** (the X), **9-3 Breakfast** (Kaze RS)

### Chapter 10: "The Night of the X". Two years earlier (coast, sunset into night, no choice)
Nico's workshop is going under. Saint offers anyone a pink-slip race for the X. Nico bets his Kaze,
which is everything he has.
- **10-1 Sunset Road**, **10-2 Saint's Line** (learn it behind Saint's ghost)
- **10-3 Pink Slip: Saint (FINAL BOSS):** gold = beat Saint. Author = Saint's legendary record.

**Cutscene:** the last hairpin over the bay. Nico's nose touches Saint's rear quarter. Saint goes
through the barrier. Nico climbs down. The X sits upright on the beach, engine running, lights on, the
passenger door dented. Saint lies on the rocks, dead. Nico gets into the X and drives away.

**Credits drive (unscored):** the X up the coast road into the night. The road bends, rain starts,
the ring road, brake lights. *Tonight.*

## 5. Finale: "Tonight, again"

The arrival you chose in chapter 1 now plays out in full. You chose where Nico *wanted* to go; the
past you built decides where he *ends up*.

Two hidden values:

| value | set by | |
|---|---|---|
| **Collection** | chapters 2, 4, 6, 8: PUSH or CLEAN | 0-4 bosses pushed |
| **Heart** | chapters 3, 5, 7: HOME or the meet/LENA | Lena chosen 2-3 times = there was an affair, and SHE was Lena; otherwise SHE was Clara |

| ending | condition | final scene |
|---|---|---|
| **THE COLLECTION** | pushed 3-4 bosses | The lockup lights come on row by row: Brute, Veloce, Kitsune, Goliath, his old Kaze. Every car belonged to a driver who "crashed". He parks the X in its bay. The bay next to it is empty. (One shot finally shows SHE's face, whoever she was. He no longer cares.) |
| **HOME** | pushed 0-2, SHE was Lena | The porch light. Clara asleep on the sofa. Ella's drawing of "Daddy's black car" on the fridge. He puts the X's key in a drawer and closes it. A happy family, kept by a lie. |
| **LAST CALL** | pushed 0-2, SHE was Clara | The house is dark, her things gone, Ella's room empty. The pub. *"Nice car outside. Yours?"* *"No. It belonged to someone else."* |

If the chapter 1 destination doesn't match the ending, the finale opens where you sent him and then
turns. He sits outside the house with the engine running and drives to the lockup. Or at the bar his
phone lights up with *"goodnight daddy"* and he leaves the beer. That's one short scene for each of
the 6 mismatches.

The theme, borrowed from *Memento*: **the lie keeps you going.** Telling the truth costs Nico his family,
and the secret keeps it.

## 6. Extras (optional)

- **Memory fragments:** each author medal unlocks one (30 in total). They are newspaper clippings,
  photos and old phone messages that fill in Saint, the bosses and Lena.
- **Secret ending "Confession":** with all 30 author medals, a fourth gate opens in map 1-3: the
  police station.
- **Chronological cut** (a nod to *Memento*'s hidden DVD mode): after the credits, replay the
  chapters in time order.
- **Unlocks:** each boss's car style (livery, rims) after their chapter, and the X as a 7th car for
  solo and online after the story.
- **Ghosts as memories:** on the coast maps a faint ghost of the X sometimes runs ahead of you.

## 7. What it needs (build list, in order)

1. **Medals** (bronze/silver/gold/author) per map and per finish gate. Author times are generated from
   recorded runs or the AutoDriver so they can be regenerated if the handling changes (the TMNF port
   on `tmnf-physics`).
2. **Forked roads with several finish gates.** The track builder has one path and one finish today
   (`game/tracks/track_builder.gd`).
3. **Story runner:** chapter flow, gold unlocks, saved choices and hidden values, endings, chapter select.
4. **Scene player:** camera shots, car moves, subtitles, phone messages.
5. **30 story maps** built around the scenes, in the existing city/stadium/coast settings.
6. **The X** (new car) and the **boss ghosts** (one recorded run per boss map).
