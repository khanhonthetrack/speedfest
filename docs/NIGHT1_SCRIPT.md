## How to read this

This is night 1 exactly as the game plays it today: five cutscenes with three races between them. The dialogue is word for word from the scene files; the action lines describe what is on screen.

- **Night 1 is the auction night.** Kit Arlen, a 20-year-old engineering student who delivers noodles, buys a black coupe at a night auction on the docks for 900. By the end of the night Kit learns it is the white car that went off the Halloran toll bridge eight years ago, painted black.
- **Order:** Prologue, then The Auction, race 1, Triple, race 2, The File, race 3 (the decision), The Yard Decides, then The Mirror and the knock.
- **Who's who:** Kit (the player). Dee Sandoval, Kit's friend, a night mechanic with a workshop called Arch 9 under the railway viaduct; on the phone for most of the night. Gus Pell, who owns the docks and runs the auction. Tibor, Gus's driver. Sgt. Voss, a traffic police sergeant. The man in the long coat (unnamed).
- **Conventions:** INT. is inside, EXT. outside. *(phone)* means the voice comes over Kit's phone; *(off)* means heard but not seen.
- **The satnav:** the car still has its old satnav. In The Auction it switches itself on and shows nine purple pins on the city map. Everything said later about "the pins" or "wiping it" means that satnav.

## Scene 1: The Auction

**EXT. THE CITY, ACROSS THE HARBOUR. NIGHT. RAIN.**

Low over the docks, past the legs of a ship-to-shore crane: across black water, the city in a lit sodium haze, a searchlight in the rain. **Title**, low on the left: NIGHT 1. *The Yard.*

**EXT. GUS PELL'S SALVAGE DOCKS, THE CONTAINER CANYON. NIGHT. RAIN.**

Behind Kit, at eye level: Kit walks down a canyon of stacked containers towards the light, the puddles on the wet quay catching the sodium lamps, a crane's gantry legs framing the city beyond.

**EXT. THE AUCTION YARD. NIGHT. RAIN.**

Over Kit's shoulder in the small crowd: Gus Pell, big, in a hi-vis vest, on a low stage under a lit truss; five cars in a row before him with lot numbers; container stacks and the PELL SALVAGE portacabin behind; a fire in an oil drum.

> **GUS**: Lot forty-six, no reserve, gone to the gentleman at the back...

The gentleman at the back: the man in the long coat and a hat, alone at the crowd's edge.

> **GUS** *(off)*: ...for twelve hundred.

Gus, on the stage.

> **GUS**: Lot forty-seven.

Kit in the crowd, hood up, backpack on, watching.

Lot 47, close, in the rain: the black coupe low from the front, its lamps, the flared arch.

> **GUS** *(off)*: Black coupe. Widebody.

Its front wheel, the violet underglow on the wet ground.

> **GUS** *(off)*: No papers, no service history, runs.

Round its tail, the tail lamps glowing.

> **GUS** *(off)*: Somebody loved this one, once.

Kit's eyes, on the car.

The paint up close along the rear quarter: black and grey camouflage, painted by hand.

> **GUS** *(off)*: Engine's sweet. Somebody painted her by hand, look at that.

Head-on and low, a light sweeping over the paint, Gus a silhouette on the stage behind.

> **GUS** *(off)*: Who'll start me?

In the crowd, behind Kit. Kit has a phone to one ear.

> **KIT**: Dee. It's black, it's wide, and it's going to go for way more than nine hundred.
>
> **DEE** *(phone)*: Is it ugly? Ugly's cheap.
>
> **KIT**: It's beautiful.
>
> **DEE** *(phone)*: Then it's not nine hundred. Walk away, student.
>
> **GUS**: Five hundred, anyone? Five. The backpack, five hundred. Six? Seven. Eight...
>
> **KIT**: He's going to eight. Nobody's...
>
> **THE MAN IN THE LONG COAT**: Two thousand.

The yard again: Gus by the cars, the crowd, the long coat at the back.

> **GUS**: Two thousand, from the long coat at the back. Two thousand. Going...
>
> **KIT**: Two thousand. Of course.
>
> **DEE** *(phone)*: Told you. Come home, I'll make you noodles that aren't your own deliveries.
>
> **GUS**: Going...

The man in the long coat turns and walks away from the yard, out into the rain, without a word.

> **GUS**: ...Sir? Sir! You can't walk off a bid, sir!
>
> **GUS**: Right. Bid's withdrawn. Back to the student. Nine hundred, kid?
>
> **KIT**: Nine hundred.
>
> **GUS**: Sold. Nine hundred, to the backpack. God help you.

**INT. THE BLACK COUPE. CONTINUOUS.**

Kit in the driver's seat for the first time. Kit turns the key. A two-note chime: the old satnav in the dash lights up by itself, a map of the city with nine purple pins on it.

> **SATNAV**: Destination not set.
>

## Race 1: Lot 47

Kit's first drive in the coupe: out of the auction yard and round the quay, about 25 seconds a lap. Like every story map it is a 7-minute time attack: the best run counts and must beat the time to beat.

## Race 2: Container Canyon

Between the container stacks, with Tibor's ghost car to beat, about 30 seconds a lap. Beating him is Kit getting away.

## Race 3: Crane Hook (the decision)

Back past the cabin and out, about 35 seconds, with four finish lines. The finish of your best run is Kit's decision, and the satnav says "Recalculating" as you cross it. The cruel choice is the straight, fastest line, so the stopwatch pulls you towards it.

| Finish | Kit's decision | Next night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main line, flat out under the swinging crane hook (fastest) | Sell Gus the name of the workshop that rebuilt the car: Dee's arch | The Lock |
| First fork, wide and safe | Pay Gus the difference out of Kit's savings | The Roof |
| Second fork | Get Gus to read the Halloran buyer a false chassis number | The Body Shop |
| Third fork | Give the recovery file to Sgt. Voss, waiting at the dock gate | The Patrol |

## Scene 4: The Yard Decides

**EXT. GUS'S CABIN, THE DOCKS. NIGHT. RAIN.**

Gus by the coupe. One of four versions plays, depending on the finish of Kit's best run.

**If Kit took the main line (sell Gus the arch's name):**

> **KIT**: Arch Nine. Under the railway viaduct. Dee Sandoval. She rebuilds things.
>
> **GUS**: That's worth something to the right people.
>
> **KIT**: She's my friend.
>
> **GUS**: Then you shouldn't have said her name.

**If Kit took the first fork (pay the difference):**

> **KIT**: Here. That's the rest of my rent. Tell your man it's sold.
>
> **GUS**: Noodles for a month, kid.
>
> **KIT**: I deliver noodles. I'll eat the mistakes.
>
> **GUS**: Fair. And the file's yours. I never had it.

**If Kit took the second fork (a false chassis number):**

> **GUS**: He'll check the number, you know. These people check everything.
>
> **KIT**: Then read him the wrong one slowly.
>
> **GUS**: Four... seven... nine... You're a bad influence, student.

**If Kit took the third fork (the file to Voss):**

> **GUS**: There's a cop at the gate. Been there since you drove in.

**EXT. THE DOCK GATE. CONTINUOUS.** Sgt. Voss, in a navy uniform coat, stands by her unmarked white car, far back past the coupe.

> **VOSS**: Gus Pell doesn't keep files. Gus Pell sells them.
>
> **KIT**: Then I'm giving you one.
>
> **VOSS**: Where did you get this car?
>
> **KIT**: Here. Tonight. Nine hundred.
>
> **VOSS**: Then you've had it about four hours too long. Drive carefully, student. Especially on bridges.

**Every version ends the same way. INT. THE COUPE.** The satnav chimes; the map swings to the next pin.

> **SATNAV**: Recalculating.

## Scene 5: The Mirror

Rewritten for take five (your notes on take four: too long and too close on the woman in the back; the bar: Call of Duty and Need for Speed cutscenes). No dialogue; about 30 seconds; the shots get shorter towards the whiteout; she is on screen for about a second in all.

**INT. THE COUPE, PARKED OUTSIDE ARCH 9. BEFORE DAWN. RAIN.**

From the dash, easing in: Kit asleep at the wheel, head against the window, neon light from outside on Kit's face.

**EXT. AND INT. A CITY STREET AT NIGHT. THE DREAM.**

- Low on a wet street between lit towers, the coupe tracks towards us through the rain; far back, a pair of headlights.
- From the passenger seat: Kit in profile, the street sliding past the window. Kit's eyes go up to the mirror.
- The mirror: the headlights, closer.
- Low on the road behind, on a long lens: the white coupe, coming.
- From the passenger side: Kit checks the mirror, then looks back over the shoulder, uneasy.
- The rear window: the white coupe right behind, its white bonnet red in Kit's tail lights, rain beading the glass.
- Its high beams flood the car. Kit squints and turns away, then is drawn back to the mirror.
- The mirror burns white. A thump on the bumper. Whiteout.
- The same mirror: an empty, dark road, Kit's tail lights on the wet tarmac.
- Kit sinks back and breathes out. Water drips somewhere in the car.
- The same mirror again: the rain has stopped. In the back seat, a woman, soaked, her hair over her face, in a cold light. A low drone.
- Kit's eyes in the mirror, finding her.
- From the back seat, where she was: Kit twists round, into the lens. Nobody there.
- The passenger seat, empty. Silence.
- Kit's eyes, turned back: her face right there, through the wet hair, one eye. The sting. Hard cut to black.
- In the black, Kit gasps awake.

## Scene 5, continued: The knock

**EXT. ARCH 9, UNDER THE RAILWAY VIADUCT. JUST AFTER SIX, STILL DARK. RAIN.**

A row of brick railway arches in the first grey light. One of them, ARCH 9 painted over it, has its roller door half up and a bare bulb burning inside: Dee's workshop. A train rumbles across the viaduct overhead. The black coupe is parked outside, the street wet. The camera comes down from high as Dee walks out to the car.

**INT. THE COUPE. CONTINUOUS.**

Seen from the dash: Kit, asleep against the side window. A hand taps on the glass: Kit wakes and turns to it. Dee is leaning in at the window outside, bent down to Kit's height, one hand on the door, the lit arch behind her. Kit winds the window down, and Dee leans on the door with both hands on the window's ledge. Then the two of them from the passenger seat; then Dee over Kit's shoulder and Kit from the dash, closer as it goes on, and closer still when Kit says what was in the mirror.

> **DEE**: Kit. Kit! Open up.
>
> **KIT**: What... what time is it?
>
> **DEE**: Gone six. You slept in it.
>
> **KIT**: I wasn't sleeping. I was driving. There was a white car right behind me.
>
> **DEE**: You were drooling on the wheel.
>
> **KIT**: There was someone in the back.
>
> **DEE**: There's a parcel shelf back there, and a wet patch. You need a new door seal, that's all.
>
> **KIT**: I saw her in the mirror. She was soaking.
>
> **DEE**: Old cars give you bad dreams. They've been places.

Close on the satnav in the dash: the city map, nine purple pins.

> **DEE** *(off)*: Huh. Nine pins.

Back in the car: Kit, turned to Dee at the window.

> **KIT**: Wipe it. Please.

Outside, the camera rising away from the car under the arch, Dee straightening up from the window.

> **DEE**: Not yet. Let's see where they go first.

End of night 1.
