SPEEDFEST STADIUM - how to play
===============================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere and run SpeedFestStadium.exe (Linux: SpeedFestStadium.x86_64).
   Nothing else to install. Windows may warn "Windows protected your PC" because the game
   isn't signed: click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
2. PLAY races SpeedFest's own campaign: 10 tracks with author, gold, silver and bronze times.
   Your best times and the ghost of your best run are saved.
3. ONLINE -> "Join the official server" races everyone else on 5-minute rounds of random tracks
   (time attack: restart as often as you like, your best time counts); or type a server's
   address there.

TrackMania tracks too: OPEN A TRACK FILE races any TrackMania Nations Forever track file
(.Challenge.Gbx), yours or downloaded. If TrackMania Nations Forever (free) is installed, its
65-track campaign shows up in PLAY as more tabs. It's found in its usual folder
(C:\Program Files (x86)\TmNationsForever or Steam's); elsewhere start the game with
-- --tmnf="D:\Games\TmNationsForever", or set the TMNF_DIR environment variable.

Straight into a track: SpeedFestStadium.exe -- --race=SF03

Keys: arrows / WASD or a gamepad to drive, Enter respawns at the last checkpoint, Backspace
restarts, Escape pauses, T chats online.

Options: graphics Low / High / Ultra (Ultra for strong graphics cards), HD textures, shadows,
sound, music and its volume, the ghost of your best run.

Music and sounds: open-licensed works by their authors; see CREDITS-music.md and
CREDITS-sounds.md. Fonts: Orbitron and Rajdhani (SIL Open Font License).
